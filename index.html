A little about me

I design things people actually use.

I'm a creative and detail-oriented UI/UX and graphic designer, based in Delhi. I craft user-centered digital experiences: responsive web and mobile interfaces, brand identities, and visual content for events and social media.

I’m a creative person who enjoys turning ideas into thoughtful digital experiences and visual work. I’m interested in how design, technology, and communication come together to make something feel clear, useful, and memorable.

My work spans UI/UX, web design, graphic design, and branding, with a strong interest in typography, composition, colour, and interaction. I like experimenting with new tools and AI-assisted workflows, while keeping the final outcome simple, purposeful, and distinctly human.
